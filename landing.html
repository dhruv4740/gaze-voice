How It Works: 4-Tier Pipeline

Zero-latency multimodal pipeline connecting patient gaze to caregiver ears.

1

Webcam Eye Tracking

Lightweight browser-based gaze estimation with magnetic snap-to-target. Selectable tiles (>200x150px) lock gaze firmly even with tremor or low lighting.

2

Ambient Listening

Web Speech API continuously monitors the room for caregiver questions in English (en-IN) or Hindi (hi-IN), displaying the heard strip instantly.

3

Dual-Tier LLM Prediction

Groq Cloud ultra-fast inference (openai/gpt-oss-20b, ~300ms) generates 6 context-aware conversational replies. Instant fallback to Google Gemini Flash-Lite.

4

Multimodal Dispatch

Speech synthesis speaks the chosen phrase aloud. The Caregiver Monitor updates live via BroadcastChannel, and emergency events trigger instant alerts.

Clinical & Accessibility Innovations

Designed for real-world hospital beds and home care environments.

Caregiver Onboarding & Memory

Patient memory stores family names, medication schedules, and past choices in local storage. Replies automatically prioritize familiar phrases and timely needs.

High-Contrast & Large Text

One-click accessibility toggles with pure black-and-gold high-contrast styling and 125% scaled typography readable from 1.5 meters away across a hospital bed.

Live Bilingual Translation

Cross-language bridging: Patient can reply in Hindi, English, or Marathi. Speech synthesis renders the listener's preferred tongue in real time.

Fatigue Detection & Rest Tiles

Listens for eye tracking fatigue signals (gaze:fatigue) and provides an immediate one-glance rest tile: "Take a break? I'm tired."

Doctor & Family Daily Summary

Summarizes daily logs into structured clinical notes: mood trends, reported pain times, home requests, and frequency counts ready to print or copy for rounds.

100% Offline Resiliency

If internet or APIs drop, the system automatically falls back to curated essential category tiles, letter spelling, and emergency alarm — zero raw errors shown.
